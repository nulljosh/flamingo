Glass

Chrome floats over the map as Liquid Glass: blur and saturate what's under it, a bright rim on top, a soft shadow below. Content is never glass; only the controls on top of it.

Voice and rules

  • Plain and short. "Mark visited", "Next up", "Where am I?". No hype, no game slang in the chrome.
  • Honest about the data. Every place says how sure we are. Pins are approximate, and the page says so.
  • No spoilers by default. Mission and chapter names stay hidden until the player turns them on.
  • Unofficial, always. Keep the not-affiliated line. No Rockstar logos, art, map imagery or the letters GTA in the name or icon.
  • No beige, no purple, no emojis, no serif.
