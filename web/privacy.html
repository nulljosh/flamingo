Privacy

Flamingo collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking.

The places you mark visited, the missions you mark done, your collectible counts and your settings are saved on your device only (in the browser's local storage, or in the app's own storage on iPhone, iPad and Mac). Clearing them in Settings, or deleting the app, removes them.

The web app caches the map data on your device so it works offline. The site is served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs.
